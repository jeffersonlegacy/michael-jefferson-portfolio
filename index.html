Tower Hour

Real-Time WebRTC Social Commerce, AI Vision Scanner & Event Engine

LIVE SHOWROOM ↗ PRIVATE REPO (REQUEST WALKTHROUGH) ✉

OPERATIONAL BOTTLENECK

Live social shopping breaks down when video stream latency drifts out of sync with active product state, when mobile viewers drop off without SMS re-engagement, or when video rendering scales poorly across hundreds of SKUs.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Built on Next.js 16 App Router and TypeScript 6, combining LiveKit WebRTC with split-storage Firebase (Firestore + Realtime Database) across 40 compiled routes and a 373-SKU catalog.

  • Multimodal AI Product Scanner & Local Remotion Worker: Combines Tesseract.js, ZXing, and Vertex AI / Gemini (/api/scan) to match photographed household goods to catalog items, paired with a local Remotion video worker and Sentry error tracking.

Jefferson Super Reader 3000

Multimodal Vision OCR & Optimal Recognition Point (ORP) RSVP Pipeline

LIVE INSTRUMENT ↗ PUBLIC REPO ↗

OPERATIONAL BOTTLENECK

Traditional PDF text extractors scramble multi-column layouts, tables, and scanned documents—injecting running headers and hyphenation artifacts into Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP) streams.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Two-stage asynchronous ingestion pipeline pairing CloudConvert job normalization with Gemini Vision multimodal extraction and a client-side ORP cadence scheduler.

  • Layout-Aware Vision Extraction: Converts arbitrary document pages into normalized frames, using Gemini Vision to extract reading-order prose while stripping margin/footer noise.
  • ORP Alignment & Cadence Weighting: Computes focal-character indices (~35% word offset) and applies dynamic frame multipliers on clause and sentence punctuation (`1.5x–2.1x`).
